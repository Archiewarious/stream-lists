## What the extension does

The extension lets a streamer keep lists of movies and games, with a queue and a history, and shows them to the
channel's viewers in a panel. Viewers can read and search the lists. Only the streamer can change them. The streamer
can look up posters and descriptions through TMDB and import auction lots from Pointauc.

The extension is free. It does not sell anything, take payments, or handle money. Pointauc points shown next to
titles are read from the streamer's Pointauc account and are only displayed.
